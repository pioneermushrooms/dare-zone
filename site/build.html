ARENA BUILDER

Build it.
Then go public.

Your draft is a private arena you own from the first minute. Change its settings, map and graphics, fly in it with friends you invite, and ask staff to make it public when you like it.

Sign in first

Drafts belong to Foundation pilots. Sign in, then come back to this page. No account yet? Claim a pilot name.

Make your draft

  1. Name it. 3–14 letters and digits, starting with a letter. It becomes #name now and name when it goes public.
  2. Build and fly. Nobody else sees it unless you invite them.
  3. Ask to go public when it's ready.

Fly in itEdit settings, map and graphics

Who can enter

Only you, your co-mods and pilots you invite (up to 5). Anyone else who tries is sent to the public arena.

    Go public

    Staff fly in your draft and decide in public. Say in a line what it's for.

    Publish an update

    Copies your draft's settings, map and graphics over the public arena and restarts it. Players in it rejoin in a second.
