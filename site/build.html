ARENA BUILDER

Build it.
Then go public.

Your draft is a private arena you own from the first minute. Change its settings, map and graphics, fly in it with friends you invite, and make it public when you like it. No approval needed.

Sign in first

Drafts belong to Foundation pilots. Sign in, then come back to this page. No account yet? Claim a pilot name.

Make your draft

  1. Name it. 3–14 letters and digits, starting with a letter. It becomes #name now and name when it goes public.
  2. Build and fly. Nobody else sees it unless you invite them.
  3. Go public when it's ready. It's live at once.

Waiting for staff

These could not go live on their own (or were asked before arenas launched without approval). Try again from your draft, or withdraw.
