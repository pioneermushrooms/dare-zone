Arena ownership

Make an arena yours

An arena is your place to bring a new game idea to life. Choose a map, set the rules, and invite other pilots to help run it.

Get started

  1. Get a pilot name. Create your account and sign in.
  2. Pitch your arena. Tell Foundation staff what you want to build from the arena dashboard.
  3. Make it yours. After approval, set your rules, upload maps and graphics, and choose up to five co-mods.

Open the arena dashboard

Running your arena

Your settings and moderation tools apply inside your arena. The public directory shows arena requests and decisions. Inactive arenas can be archived and brought back later.

What can owners change?

Owners can edit their arena’s settings, replace its map, manage its graphics, and appoint co-mods. Co-mods can help with settings and moderation.

What can moderators do?

Run timers, manage teams and ships, and moderate chat in the arena. Zone staff handle kicks, bans, and zone-wide announcements.

What happens to inactive arenas?

After 30 days without a visit, an empty arena can be archived. Staff can reopen it later.
