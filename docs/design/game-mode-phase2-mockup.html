Fast CTF, ready to apply

Two teams of up to 8, kept even, no Sharks. Carry all 6 flags for 3 minutes to win: 5,000 points. Kills score the killed pilot's bounty. 30-minute rounds with warnings at 1 minute, 30 and 10 seconds; nothing happens at zero yet. Fast pace, standard items.
